# MOM-ROUTE-002

Cell-controlled reading through MA-001 SQLite Registry and the frozen Raw
Hybrid router. Selected Cells determine the complete record input; global
Top 16 is a separate comparison arm. The protocol deliberately does not
apply a new evidence classifier or remove conflicting records. Negative
queries are counted separately and do not receive fabricated answer scores.

arm; the Oracle arm is the correct-only control. Native expert selections
are logged separately. See [protocol](PROTOCOL.md).
