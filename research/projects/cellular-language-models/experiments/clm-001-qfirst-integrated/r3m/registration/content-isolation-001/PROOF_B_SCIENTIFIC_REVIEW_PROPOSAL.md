# Proof B — conservative historical source exclusion: scientific review proposal

Status: PROPOSAL_ONLY_NOT_APPROVED. No active gate or scientific config is changed. The R3h B-H sampling-boundary work is closed and is not repeated here. Its CONSISTENT/NOT_CERTIFIED status remains separate from the data-isolation question.

## Proposed claim and containment obligations

Let H be all model-visible historical content potentially relevant to R3m evaluation: training, tokenizer training, calibration, development/validation, accepted and abandoned Proposal/Shadow/case branches, plus explicitly reserved Confirmation content for role separation. Let U be a verified conservative union of those sources under their original tokenizers, preprocessing and boundary rules. Let Q be the fixed512 Main candidates and their intended input/context/label spans.

Proof B would require both (1) H is contained in U and (2) Q has no overlap with U under a separately approved content/fragment predicate. Larger U is conservative only when its identity and membership are known; absent metadata cannot simply be called covered. Unbounded reservations are neither silently infinite nor silently empty.

A valid source-union proof could establish data isolation without recovering every actual optimizer start. This would not retroactively certify exact historical reproduction or establish the original NumPy version. Any new `DATA_ISOLATION_BY_VERIFIED_SOURCE_SUPERSET` decision must be an explicit approved protocol amendment, not a new interpretation of current PASS flags.

## Evidence levels that must remain distinct

1. Exact original manifest, tokenizer and source bytes/IDs/window metadata.
2. Exact model-visible corpus byte reconstruction under an original tokenizer.
3. Verified frozen loader/config source bounds, whose actual run artifacts remain missing.
4. Declarative index ranges with snapshot identity but unverified runtime membership/index convention.
5. Missing identities, unknown reservation extents and unpublished runtime/manual source choices.

The58 imported unpinned claims are mapped individually to levels2/3 and the reused007 proof; original declarations remain unchanged. The broader181-claim ledger includes123 pinned declarations. Counts of declarations are not counts of independently trained models. The ledger itself does not prove completeness for unpublished or external runtime data.

## Fragment audit interpretation

The independently frozen engineering diagnostic uses exact16/32/64/65-token fragments in each original tokenizer view and16/32/64 normalized-word fragments. It includes all stride-1 positions, explicit story boundaries and complete terminal-story over-exclusion. Hash hits require full elementwise equality; reporting is per view/length, with no candidate replacement or threshold adaptation.

Within one tokenizer view, a match identifies an exact shared sequence in the declared source universe. This does not establish that that corpus location was actually sampled by an optimizer, nor does a short common phrase automatically invalidate a model-quality estimate. Those are separate scientific interpretations. Any observed matches remain in evidence and prevent an unqualified assertion of zero fragment overlap.

Equal numeric lengths across tokenizers are not equal raw-text spans. The current cross-view diagnostic is not a complete byte-aligned proof of equivalence under arbitrary historical preprocessing. A stronger context-specific claim needs original token-to-text span maps: each actual intended Main input-plus-label span must be projected to each relevant historical representation with consistent EOS/context handling and checked against the verified historical source universe. Unmodeled preprocessing, paraphrases or fragments shorter than diagnostic lengths are not certified absent.

## Required independent decisions

- Is U complete for all relevant historical exposures and reserved roles? Which conditional source bounds are acceptable, and which require actual manifests?
- How are the14 unbounded reservation markers resolved from original declarations/owner review? No new extent may be invented by the implementer.
- What exact full-window/partial-fragment/normalization predicate is the scientific isolation requirement? The diagnostic rules were frozen before inspection; any later criterion must openly acknowledge the already observed audit and cannot be tuned merely to release these candidates.
- Are all relevant tokenizer/preprocessing variants represented, including cross-story source windows and full input/label context?
- If a candidate has overlap, what scientific conclusion follows? This task does not remove or replace candidates.
- If a complete proof is later accepted, explicitly revise the data-isolation gate while leaving historical-reproduction version certification unchanged.
