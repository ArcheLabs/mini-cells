# What this experiment does not prove

- Exact causal KV reuse is not learned event sparsity or fixed-capacity memory.
- Synthetic event speed is not language-model end-to-end speed.
- Engineering parity on random tiny fixtures is not recovered-model certification.
- FP32 real-weight parity is not FP16/BF16/CUDA coverage.
- A0/A1 speed differences do not establish a modern T1 competitive advantage.
- Different E0 scales, quality metrics, devices and phases cannot be merged.
- Three-iteration/stage caches do not permit unlimited position extrapolation.
- No new model has been trained and no historical result has been changed.
- H1, H2 and H3 require separate decisions. Missing evidence stays INCOMPLETE.
