# Foundations: Echo, NCA Language Dynamics, and Training Mechanics

**Stage question:** Why try cellular language dynamics, and which NCA properties transfer to language?

Experiments 001–013 moved from Echo and basic trainability through 1D/2D latent tissue, adaptive halting, settling dynamics, stabilization costs, and random-depth controls. They tested local recurrent state transitions as a language substrate and established reusable training, evaluation, and reproducibility machinery.

The work produced historical support for cellular language dynamics, but scaling, stable settling, and efficient training remained difficult. Neither local dynamics nor a 2D tissue demonstrated true continual learning. What survived was the emphasis on local state, repeated computation, controlled dynamics, and explicit mechanistic tests.
