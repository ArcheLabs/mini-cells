# Self-Organization: Tissue Formation, Recruitment, Differentiation, and Trait Genesis

**Stage question:** Can useful functional organization emerge rather than be fully hand-designed?

Experiments 014–024 studied multi-seed stabilization, emergent sparse topology, reaction–diffusion-like plasticity, growing cellular models, localized learning, tissue recruitment, capability specificity, conflict-driven differentiation, trait genesis, and probationary genesis.

The stage yielded interesting emergent organization and practical mechanisms for localized recruitment and differentiation. It also exposed numerical fragility, null modes, and the gap between visually or behaviorally interesting emergence and a validated continual-learning mechanism. The surviving ideas were sparse topology, local mutability, conditional recruitment, probation, and growth under pressure.

Canonical evidence: [`014-multiseed-core-recipe`](../../../artifacts/experiments/014-multiseed-core-recipe) through [`024b-independent-third-trait`](../../../artifacts/experiments/024b-independent-third-trait). Detailed historical documents are retained in [`sources/`](sources/).
