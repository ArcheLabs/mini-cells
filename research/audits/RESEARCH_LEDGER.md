# MiniCells Global Research Ledger

Audit date: **2026-09-03**

This ledger is a family-level audit of experiments with durable scientific or engineering value. It does not replace frozen protocol/result files. Its job is to preserve the meaning of those results after the active roadmap changes.

## Status vocabulary

- **SUPPORTED — controlled**: registered claim passed, but only inside an explicit synthetic/linear/scaffolded boundary.
- **SUPPORTED — mechanism/diagnostic**: a narrower mechanism fact is supported; this is not a continual-learning milestone.
- **PARTIAL**: useful causal or quantitative evidence exists, while the registered system-level claim failed or remains unresolved.
- **NOT SUPPORTED**: frozen registered claim failed.
- **INCOMPLETE / ACTIVE**: no final scientific decision should be inferred.

## Core 005 — replay-free subspace-certified mitosis

**Claim**

## Core 006 — real-representation continual plasticity

**Claim**  
Can the Core-005 style bounded state support replay-free continual learning on real pretrained language-model representations without rapid saturation or growth explosion?

**Evidence source**  
`research/validations/core-006-real-representation-continual-plasticity/README.md`.

**Positive evidence**
- real Pythia representations did not immediately fill the 64-D projected space;
- functional reuse increased;
- certificate writes reduced registered-history forgetting relative to unsafe writes;
- candidate retained substantial new-learning gain relative to replay;
- spawned children were reused.

**Negative evidence**
- `REAL_REPRESENTATION_CONTINUAL_PLASTICITY_NOT_SUPPORTED`, 0/3 formal seeds;
- spawned Cells covered ~65.6–68.8% of addresses, above the registered bound;
- address-based splits failed the registered conflict-relief requirement;

**Formal status**  
**NOT SUPPORTED**, with positive sub-results.

**Known ceiling**  
Real hidden-state low-dimensional/reusable geometry is useful, but routing address is not equivalent to a writable functional boundary.

**What it does NOT prove**  
That semantic addresses are Cells, that growth remains bounded, or that the certificate mechanism alone solves continual learning.

**Engineering primitive obtained**  
Real-model functional regression measurement and certificate-based damage reduction.

## Cross-experiment conclusions that survive the audit

1. **Local protection is real but incomplete.** Safe subspace mechanisms can causally reduce damage, yet they do not establish globally successful continual language learning.
2. **Capacity and addressing are separate resources.** New writable capacity can exist while read ownership remains unsafe.
3. **Synthetic mechanism existence and trained-model integration must remain separate claims.** Constructive CLM is a valid existence stack; Native M2/M3/M3R define its current transfer ceiling.
4. **Optimizer mechanics are not semantics.** R0b closes an update-transaction issue but cannot rescue a failed continual-learning criterion.
5. **Local evaluation cannot certify global improvement.** The engineering system should generate isolated candidates locally and accept model versions only after broader stage-level evaluation.
6. **The current near-term value of Cells is engineering modularity.** A Cell may be a deliberately chosen unit of model change, validation, versioning and rollback without claiming it is a natural knowledge atom.

When a new experiment becomes summary-worthy, append/update one family entry here only after its frozen result is published. Never remove a negative result because a later mechanism changes the roadmap.
