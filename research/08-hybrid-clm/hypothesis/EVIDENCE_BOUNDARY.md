# HybridCLM evidence boundary

## Software capability

The v0.1 toolkit can cellularize supported Granite MoE locations, attach and
detach compatible Cell mutations, scale their expression reversibly, validate
artifact identity, and restore the cellularized baseline.

## Engineering evidence

PCU Hybrid Reattachment 001 v3 reports: same-cellular zero-state equivalence
passed; Cell OFF ranking 6.25%; Cell ON ranking 82.03%; causal hybrid
consumption and exact restoration were supported. The alpha=1 locality
threshold failed, no pre-registered jointly passing amplitude point was found,
and formal validation has not run.

## Not established

The evidence does not establish formal HybridCLM validation, general
model-family transfer, standalone CLM conversion, superiority to LoRA/PEFT,
general continual learning, or bounded long-term interference.
